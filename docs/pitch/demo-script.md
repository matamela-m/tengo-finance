# Three-minute demo script (build 2)

Prepared 2026-10-21. Replaces the script in `docs/review/judge-qa.md`. Every screen named here exists in build 2. Timing in brackets. Rehearse with a stopwatch; cut the line in italics if you run long.

1. **Hook (0:00 to 0:20).** "A balance tells you what you have. Tengo tells you until when." One sentence on who: people paid by the trip, the client or the till, not on the 25th.

2. **Thabo, Tight (0:20 to 0:50).** Open Thabo's Home. Point to "Tight until", the likely range and next money on Monday. "The headline, budget and next money on this screen follow one rule: the state." Open Why this date for two seconds: the inputs in plain words.

3. **Ayanda, Short (0:50 to 1:20).** Switch to Ayanda. "Short from Friday, two days before her next money. The daily budget says Not advised, not a number that contradicts it." Show What helps most: earn or receive more, with the borrowing caution. "It is built not to suggest skipping a car instalment or cutting stock."

4. **When the pattern breaks (1:20 to 1:45).** *Optional if short on time.* Say: "After two missed pay gaps, Tengo says Income looks paused and stops counting on it. With no income or two weeks of history, it says Not enough to tell." Show one of these on Use your own money with sample numbers if time allows.

5. **Your own money (1:45 to 2:20).** Back to the picker, Use your own money, Try with sample numbers: a forecast in about a minute, no sign-up, nothing leaves the phone. Point to the chip "Your profile | This device only". Add R150 cash out in one tap and watch the budget change.

6. **Trust (2:20 to 2:40).** Quick exit and discreet mode in one tap each. "Built for shared phones." Privacy: consent per purpose, export, real deletion with a receipt. Optional account with a one-time code, shown as a demo path, not yet for real data.

7. **Evidence and model (2:40 to 3:00).** One slide: on our synthetic backtest, counting small income, the date is off by 3.2 days against 5.9 for a simple baseline; counting outflows only, the baseline wins. "This is data we generated and tuned on, so it shows consistency, not real-world accuracy. Real statements come next." Business: a bank licence of R5 per active user a month with a pilot fee and an annual minimum; next steps a six-person comprehension test and a bank pilot with a control group.

**Do not say:** real users, real accuracy, "about 80 percent", "never" about harmful suggestions, compliance, partnership with any bank, or that 11 languages are reviewed. Say "11 languages: English plus 10 AI drafts that speakers still need to check."

**If something breaks:** stay on Thabo and Ayanda (seeded, offline-ready after first load), skip steps 4 and 5.
