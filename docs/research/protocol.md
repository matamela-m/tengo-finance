# Comprehension test: protocol

Prepared 2026-10-21 for the founder to run. Nothing here has been run; there are no results yet.

## Question

Do people paid irregularly read Tengo's date, likely range and cover states (Enough, Tight, Short, Not enough to tell) the way the app means them, and does the date-first Home help them decide what to do with money today?

This tests a hypothesis (BEH-08, DATA-07): that a date ("Tight until Tue 27 Oct") is easier to act on than a balance. It is not a claim.

## Participants: six people

| # | Earns from | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | E-hailing or minibus driving | Weekly payouts plus daily cash, like Thabo |
| 2 | Hair, nails or beauty | Busy weekends, quiet weekdays, like Lerato |
| 3 | Spaza or small shop | Lumpy deposits and stock runs, like Ayanda |
| 4 | Domestic or gardening work | Several employers, cash and EFT |
| 5 | Informal trading or piece work | Daily cash, often unbanked days |
| 6 | Any of the above, aged 50 or older | Checks the design beyond the young and confident |

Mix: at least three women, at least two people whose home language is not English, at least one person who uses a phone shared with family. Adults only (18 or older). Nobody who works for Tengo, a bank or a fintech.

Recruit through community networks, not the founder's friends. Offer a fair thank-you (for example data or airtime of a set value, decided before recruiting and the same for everyone, paid whether or not the session is finished).

## Setup

- 45 minutes per person, in a quiet place the participant chooses, or on a video call.
- A phone with the latest build at its deployed address, in English for the scored tasks and questions, with an interpreter for the session if wanted. The other 10 languages are unreviewed AI drafts, so their wording errors would mix with design problems in the scores. After the scored part, a participant may switch to their own language and say what reads wrong; note that separately and pass it to the translators.
- Use the **fictional personas and sample numbers only**. Never ask for or show the participant's own money.
- Two people: a facilitator who asks, and a note-taker who writes on `note-sheet.md`. No video. Audio only with consent, deleted after notes are checked.
- Run sessions 1 and 2, fix anything that blocks comprehension, then run 3 to 6 without changes so their results compare.

## Analysis

Score every answer with `scoring-guide.md`. Report per question how many of six people read it correctly, quote words people used (no names), and list the three biggest misreadings with the screen that caused them. Six people find the big problems; they do not give percentages to quote as rates.

## Ethics and data

- Written or recorded verbal consent before anything else; people may stop at any time and still get the thank-you.
- Notes use participant numbers, never names or phone numbers. Keep the key that links numbers to contact details apart from the notes and delete it after the thank-you is paid.
- Do not ask about debts, income amounts or anything the tasks do not need.
- If someone becomes distressed about their own money, stop the task, offer the free help listed on `/safety`, and do not record what they said.
