# Tengo brand assets

Prepared 2026-10-24 from the founder's supplied logo files. The logo artwork is unchanged. Nothing was redrawn, recoloured or reshaped. Padding was trimmed, single-colour variants were generated by recolouring the existing shapes, and icons were generated by scaling the supplied mark.

## Colours in the logo

The logo already uses the app's blues, so no recolouring was needed:

- Deep blue bar: `#1D4ED8` (Tailwind blue-700)
- Light blue bar: `#60A5FA` (Tailwind blue-400)
- Dark lettering and top bar: `#141416`
- Light lettering and top bar: `#FFFFFF`

Contrast of the logo blues (for reference, a logo is not body text):

| Colour | On #171717 (dark bg) | On #262626 (card) | On #FAFAFA (light bg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| #1D4ED8 | 2.68 | 2.26 | 6.42 |
| #60A5FA | 7.05 | 5.95 | 2.44 |
| #3B82F6 | 4.87 | 4.11 | 3.52 |

So `#1D4ED8` works on light backgrounds and `#60A5FA` on dark ones. That is why there are separate on-light and on-dark lockups.

## Token recommendation

Change the dark-theme accent token from `#1E3A8A` to `#1D4ED8` so the interface accent matches the logo exactly. Keep `#3B82F6` as primary and `#60A5FA` for small text and links on dark surfaces.

These keep the exact shapes and only change the fill colour.

**icons/** app and browser icons, all scaled from the supplied mark:

- `icon-dark-{16,32,180,192,512,1024}.png`: the real mark on `#171717`
- `icon-light-{16,32,180,192,512,1024}.png`: the real mark on white

- `favicon.ico`: 16, 32 and 48px, scaled from the supplied mark

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the top bar on every side.
- Minimum width for the horizontal lockup is 120px on screen.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects to, or rearrange the bars.
- Use the on-light files on light surfaces and the on-dark files on dark surfaces. Do not place the on-light lockup on a dark background.
- Over a photo or a busy background, use a mono version.
- No SVG was supplied, so do not scale the PNGs above their native size. The horizontal lockup at 1583px wide is enough for any web header. Ask the designer for SVGs when convenient.

## Small sizes

Below about 32px the three thin bars start to merge and the mark goes soft. Nothing here changes the artwork to compensate. If you want a crisper favicon, ask the designer for a small-size version of the mark with heavier bars, keeping the straight left edges on the middle and bottom bars. Until then the scaled icons are fine at 32px and above.
