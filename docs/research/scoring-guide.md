# Scoring guide

Score each answer 2 (correct), 1 (partly) or 0 (wrong or no idea). Score the meaning, not the wording. Two people score separately and agree differences.

| Q | 2: correct | 1: partly | 0: wrong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Names the date as when money runs out | Names the date but not what it means | Reads it as a payday, a due date or a balance |
| Q2 | No: next money is not counted | Unsure, but reasons about it | Yes, it counts the next payment |
| Q3 | Money is about used up around then | Says something happens, unclear what | Thinks money must be spent by then |
| Q4 | A range of likely dates, not exact | Knows it is about dates, not how sure | Thinks it is two payments or two bills |
| Q5 | Possible but less likely | Unsure | Says impossible |
| Q6 | Covered until next money even on a heavy week | Covered, without the heavy-week part | Has lots of money, can spend freely |
| Q7 | Fine on a normal week, not on a heavy one | Some risk, unclear which | Same as Enough, or same as Short |
| Q8 | Runs out before next money; no daily figure advised | Knows it is bad, not why there is no figure | Reads Not advised as an error or a ban |
| Q9 | No money coming in was found, so no date can be worked out | Something is missing | Thinks money has run out |
| Q10 | An even spread of what is there until next money; a most, not a target | Knows it is a limit, not what it is based on | Thinks it must be spent or is money given |
| Q11 | How much could go without dropping a level; says nothing about getting it back | One of the two parts | Thinks it is safe or will come back |
| Q12 | Anyone with the phone; nothing is sent in the demo | Knows it is private, not the shared-phone risk | Thinks a bank or Tengo sees it |
| Q13 | Finds Why this date or Question this result | Would ask someone | Would ignore it or delete the app |

## Tasks

| Task | Success | Partly | No |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Date and range or confidence | Date only | Balance or nothing |
| T2 | On Home, uses budget or state and says RX is more than advised; not the date as a deadline | Uses the balance only | Treats the date as permission to spend |
| T3 | Uses Plan, reads the effect and the caution | Uses Plan, misses the caution | Does not find Plan or decides on the balance |

## Reporting

For each question and task: how many of six scored 2, 1 and 0. A question or task with two or more scores of 0 is a design problem to fix before the pilot; one score of 0 is noted and watched. For T2, report how many answers changed between the balance-only screen and Home, and in which direction; with six people this shows a direction, not a proof. Report quotes without names. Do not turn six people into percentages.
