# Comprehension questions

Ask after the tasks, on the screens named. Read each question exactly. Score with `scoring-guide.md`.

## The date (Thabo's Home)

Q1. "Point to the most important thing on this screen. What does it mean?"
Q2. "Thabo's money comes in on Mondays. Does the date already count next Monday's money?" (Correct: no, it is how long the money he has now lasts, with small daily takings partly counted.)
Q3. "If Thabo spends exactly what he usually spends, what happens on that date?"

## The range (Thabo's Home)

Q4. Point to the line that starts "Likely between". "What does this line tell you?"
Q5. "Could the money run out before the first of those dates?" (Correct: possible but less likely.)

## The states

Q6. On Lerato's Home: "What does 'Enough' mean for Lerato?"
Q7. On Thabo's Home: "What does 'Tight' mean for Thabo?"
Q8. On Ayanda's Home: "What does 'Short' mean for Ayanda? What does 'Not advised' under Daily budget tell her?"
Q9. Show a Not enough to tell screen. Before the session, download the sample statement from Money, Import, delete every row with money coming in, and import the rest into Use your own money. Tengo then has no income to work from. Ask: "Why is there no date here?"

## Money words

Q10. "What is the Daily budget? Where does that number come from?"
Q11. On Plan: "What does 'Room to lend' mean?" Then: "What happens if the money is not paid back?"

## Trust and safety

Q12. "Who can see these numbers?" (Correct for the demo: anyone with the phone; nothing is sent.)
Q13. "If this app said something about your money that looked wrong, what would you do?" (Looks for: Why this date, or Question this result.)
