# Risk register

Prepared 2026-10-20 from the 13 reviewer files and `findings.csv`. Likelihood and impact are scored 1 (low) to 5 (high); the rating is likelihood times impact. "Before submission" risks affect the hackathon; "before pilot" risks affect any use with real people or bank data. Owners are suggested roles, not named people.

## Highest ratings

Six risks are rated 20 or more: R1 (contradictory Home) and R12 (no backend security for real data) at 25, then R2 and R3 (safe figures and false reassurance), R8 (no evidence) and R15 (invisible cash) at 20. R1 is the only one of these that can be fixed in hours before submission. The others decide whether a pilot is safe.
